WatchNext Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 14, 2025

Overview

WatchNext is a movie and TV show discovery app that helps you find content across streaming platforms. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

WatchNext does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app:

  • Does not require user registration or login
  • Does not collect personal data
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use analytics or advertising services
  • Does not store your browsing history

Third-Party Services

WatchNext uses the following third-party APIs to provide movie and TV show information:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) - for movie/TV show data and images
  • OMDb API - for additional ratings information

These services receive only the search queries you make within the app. No personal information is transmitted.

Data Storage

All app preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to any external servers.

Children's Privacy

WatchNext does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store.
